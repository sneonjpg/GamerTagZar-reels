The rules that protect your money

Learned the hard way, so you don't have to.

30-minute submit window

Late links are rejected. Paid clips post on approval, never on a timer while you're away.

Disclosure on screen

A small "#ad" at the start of every paid clip. Caption-only can be rejected.

No cutscenes or spoilers

Publishers take them down. Every clip is screened before you see it.

Platform limits handled

Instagram bitrate caps, YouTube's new-channel upload limit, same-language streamers only.
