## Current Rules

### Rules from Neo (2026-11-20)
- Hooks sound like a secret or insider info: "I just found out...", "Did you know...", "Nobody's talking about...", "Wait... GTA 6 has X?". Curiosity first, always true to the clip.
- Clips 30 seconds or longer (watch time). Auto cuts now aim for 30-45s.
- Never post story cutscenes during launch week.
- Approved clips post spaced about 1 hour apart, YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels.

### 2026-11-20 results and changes
- KreekCraft clips: ~3,000 views on one Instagram Reel on day one. Instagram > YouTube so far.
- Balance: campaign streamers are recorded 40 min for 8 clips per run; growth streamers 20 min for 4 clips, top 3 only.
- Posting order: campaign clips first, a growth clip between them, about 1 per hour.
- Campaign clips get a small "#ad" label on screen for the first 2 seconds (Content Rewards wants a clear disclosure at the start).
- YouTube titles lead with the hook; "#ad" goes at the end of the title and in the first description line.

### Submission rule (from Neo, 2026-11-20)
- Every campaign clip: the moment it posts, fetch its Instagram (and YouTube) link and send Neo a nudge with the link ready to copy plus the campaign submit link. Content Rewards wants submission within 30 minutes of posting.
- Link lookup: Firecrawl Alexandria scrapecreators instagram/user-reels, handle "gamertagzar" (2 credits).
- Day 1 Instagram views: keyless car 2,532 · jet 1,992 · run over 243 · parachute 239 · yard sale 189.

### 2026-11-21 growth-clip experiments
- Growth clips now get emoji stickers on key words (money, cops, bank, crash...) and an end-card comment question. Campaign clips stay clean.
- Compare growth clip views/comments before vs after this change over the next week.
- 2026-11-21: Every Instagram post now gets an automatic first comment with a reply prompt (Make step). Instagram's API can't pin comments, so pinning stays manual (optional).
- Captions end with a question ("Would you try this? 👇").
