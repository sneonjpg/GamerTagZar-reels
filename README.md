# GamerTagZar

Everything for @GamerTagZar (YouTube Shorts, X, TikTok and Instagram Reels, UK time). Nothing here is shared with other channels.

- `shorts/`: GamerTagZar's own Shorts (01: GTA 6 unlock times).
- `page/`: playbook, facts, campaigns, scoreboard, payouts, media.
- `clip-desk/`: the GTA 6 clip tool (finds moments, cuts vertical clips, checks rules). It feeds GamerTagZar; it is not a separate page.

## Make clips from your phone (free)

1. Open https://github.com/sneonjpg/GamerTagZar-reels/actions/workflows/make-clips.yml
2. Tap **Run workflow**, paste a stream or video link, tap the green **Run workflow**.
3. In about 25 to 40 minutes the clips appear in `page/media/edits/<date>-run<number>/` with a `clips.md` summary.

It runs on GitHub's computers, so nothing is needed on your phone. Nothing is posted: you choose what to post.
